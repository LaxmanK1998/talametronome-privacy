**Privacy Policy for TalaMetronome**

Last Updated: August 10, 2026
Kamatsoft ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information and data.
1. Information We Collect
We believe in minimal data collection. We only collect information that you explicitly choose to provide to us:
•	Feedback and Bug Reports: When you use the in-app feedback feature, the information you enter (including the selected flair, title, and description) is transmitted to us to help us troubleshoot bugs and improve the application.
•	Audio and Device Permissions:
o	Microphone Access (Optional): If you use interactive audio features like pitch tuning or audio recording, microphone permission is required. Audio data captured by your microphone is processed locally on your device and is never recorded, stored remotely, or transmitted to any external servers.
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected feedback solely to:
•	Diagnose technical issues and fix software bugs.
•	Improve user experience and add requested features.
•	Respond to user inquiries or support requests.
We do not sell, rent, or trade your feedback or any personal information to third parties or advertising networks.
3. Third-Party Services
To receive and process user feedback, our application connects to third-party infrastructure:
•	Google Services (Google Apps Script / Google Sheets): Submissions sent through our feedback form are processed using secure Google APIs and stored in an administrative Google Sheet to manage support tickets. You can review Google’s Privacy Policy for more details on how Google processes data.
4. Data Security & Retention
We implement reasonable technical measures to ensure that your feedback submissions are handled securely. Feedback data is retained only as long as necessary to review and resolve the reported issues or implement feature suggestions, after which it may be deleted.
5. Children’s Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
