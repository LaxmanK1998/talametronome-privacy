# Privacy Policy for TalaMetronome

**Last Updated:** August 20, 2026

**Kamatsoft** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, **TalaMetronome**, handles user information and data.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We believe in strict minimal data collection. We only handle information that is necessary for app functionality or explicitly provided by you:

* **Feedback and Bug Reports:** When you use the in-app feedback feature, the information you enter (including category flair, title, description, app version, and optional email) is transmitted to us to help us troubleshoot issues and improve the application.
* **Audio and Device Permissions:**
  * **Microphone Access (Optional):** Required only if you choose to use the interactive **Pitch Tuner**, **Raga Detector**, or **Studio Audio Recorder** features. Audio captured by your microphone is processed strictly in real-time on your local device. Practice recordings are saved exclusively in your device's local storage and are never uploaded, recorded remotely, or transmitted to any external servers.
  * **Proximity Sensor (Optional):** Used locally on your device for hands-free gesture control (such as waving your hand over the sensor to start/pause playback or trigger a drum fill). Proximity sensor data is evaluated in real-time on-device and is never stored, logged, or transmitted.
  * **Device Storage Access (Optional):** Used solely to save practice audio recordings (.m4a/.wav) and app preferences locally on your device.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected feedback data solely to:

* Diagnose technical issues and fix software bugs.
* Improve user experience, performance, and add requested features.
* Respond to user support inquiries.

We **do not** collect personal telemetry, we **do not** track your usage across other apps, and we **do not** sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties or advertising networks.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services

To process user-submitted feedback, our application connects to third-party infrastructure:

* **Google Services (Google Apps Script / Google Sheets API):** Submissions sent through our in-app feedback form are processed using secure Google APIs and stored in an administrative Google Sheet to manage support tickets. You can review [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on how Google processes data.

---

### 4. Data Security & Retention

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to ensure your feedback submissions are handled securely over HTTPS. Feedback data is retained only as long as necessary to review and resolve reported issues or implement feature suggestions, after which it is deleted. All local audio recordings and app settings remain strictly under your control on your personal device.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
